What we collect

The details you type into a leasing, site-visit or broker enquiry form: typically name, company, designation, business category, phone number, email address, space required and your message. The form says which are optional.

How it is used

Only to answer your enquiry: to call or message you, send floor options and the information pack, and arrange a site visit. We do not sell personal data and we do not use it for unrelated marketing.

Where it goes

The form sends your enquiry to a small handler on this website's own hosting. The handler emails the leasing team and keeps a private copy on the server so that an enquiry is not lost if an email fails. If the handler is unavailable, the form falls back to FormSubmit, a third-party form-delivery service, which emails the leasing inbox. Access is limited to the SHRINEVA leasing team.

Anonymous usage statistics, such as which pages are viewed, may be collected once an analytics service is configured on this site. They do not include the contents of your enquiry.
